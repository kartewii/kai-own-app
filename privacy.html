Privacy

Kai is a personal dashboard used only by its owner.

When the owner connects his Google account, Kai reads, labels and drafts mail in his Gmail with the permission he grants (gmail.modify). It sends mail only when he presses Send.

The mail is read on his own computer. It is not sold, shared, or stored on any other server, and it is not used to train AI models. A short copy of recent threads is kept on his computer so the dashboard works offline.

He can remove the access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
